Our Heritage & Craft

Where Artistry Meets Pastel Luxury

Founded with a passion for subtle elegance and high-end aesthetics, Sketch Beauty Salon has evolved into a sanctuary of beauty, self-care, and transformations. We believe that true beauty shines when enhancement is personalized to your unique features.

Our salon brings together certified hair couture artists, HD airbrush makeup experts, and skin dermatological estheticians operating in an atmosphere designed around soothing pastel hues and supreme comfort.

Premium Organic Formulations: We exclusively use cruelty-free, dermatologist-approved luxury products.
Tailored Consultations: Every makeover or treatment begins with a dedicated face and hair consultation.
Hygienic & Serene Environment: Sterilized tools, private bridal suites, and peaceful ambient atmosphere.
